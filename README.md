## ◈ Flagship — NeuroTrace

### 🧠 NeuroTrace — A Stroke Recovery Monitor That Lives in Your Phone
`2026` · 🥈 **Runner-up, Samsung Solve for Tomorrow 2026 — 2nd out of 39,000+ applications nationwide**

After a stroke, recovery is tracked in clinic visits weeks apart. Decline between those visits goes unseen. **NeuroTrace watches every day — using only a phone.**

```text
 45-sec phone session ──►  🎙 Voice features      ─┐
 (camera + mic)       ──►  😐 Facial movement     ─┼──►  Per-patient baseline  ──►  Cross-modal  ──►  📲 WhatsApp alert
                      ──►  ⚡ Reaction time       ─┘     (learned over 35 days)     agreement gate      to caregiver (Hindi)
```

- **Personal, not population, baselines** — every patient is compared against *their own* 35-day normal, not a textbook average
- **Two-modality agreement gate** — an alert fires only when deviations **co-occur in two independent signals**. One noisy channel can't cry wolf. That's how you build something caregivers don't learn to ignore.
- **Built for Bharat** — no wearables, no app installs for the family; alerts arrive in **Hindi on WhatsApp**, where caregivers already are

**Reinforcement Learning That Teaches Small Models to Reason** · `2026`

Frontier reasoning is locked behind clusters. AXIOM brings RL fine-tuning down to **a single GPU**.
